# ByteSized Careers legal data audit

Status: local pre-publication audit

Audit date: 17 July 2026

Repository: `guhanp09/bytesized-careers-waitlist`
Scope: the current early-access website, not a launched marketplace

This is a product and data-processing inventory, not legal advice or a compliance certification. It reflects code, schema, migrations, installed providers, and local behavior inspected on the audit date. No production system or personal lead value was accessed. Aggregate inspection of the local PostgreSQL database found zero local leads.

## Confirmed operator and policy decisions

- Public brand: ByteSized Careers.
- Operator: Guhan Purushothaman. ByteSized Careers is currently operated by Guhan Purushothaman.
- Operator/contact location: Srinagar Colony, Saidapet, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. This is not described as a registered office.
- Privacy, legal, opt-out and grievance contact: `legal@bytesizedcareers.com`.
- Eligibility: 18 or older; no date-of-birth collection.
- Governing law and venue: laws of India; competent courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, subject to mandatory rights that cannot be excluded.
- Retention: purpose-based rather than a fixed automatic waitlist deletion period. Technical verification/resume expiry remains disclosed.
- Future invitation: selected early-access fields may be pre-filled, but the user must review, correct and submit before activation; no automatic public profile or visibility.
- Communications: verification, administration, material changes, launch/access invitations, incomplete-registration reminders and future relevance notices as disclosed. Phone collection is not phone-marketing consent.
- Optional email suppression: manual operating process through `legal@bytesizedcareers.com` before any optional campaign; suppression is distinct from deletion.

## How to read the inventory

The tables use all 15 requested columns. “Basis” is a ground to evaluate for a particular purpose and visitor—not a conclusion that a named foreign law applies universally. “Current retention” records implemented behavior. “Proposed retention” records the adopted purpose-based approach and targeted technical minimisation recommendations, not a promise of automatic deletion after a fixed period.

## Operational capability and mismatch notes

- Access/correction/deletion: technically possible through administrator review and manual SQL/CSV operations, but no verified request workflow or user-facing portal exists.
- Per-subject export: the admin CSV exports all leads; it is not a data-subject access package and should not be sent without manual minimisation.
- Unsubscribe: schema fields exist, but no current public unsubscribe mechanism or marketing sender exists. “Unsubscribe anytime” was therefore unsupported and has been removed from the local draft.
- Phone: current behavior validates and saves a number plus three independent, versioned choices. No OTP or outbound message/call is sent, and recording a choice does not activate a campaign.
- Logs: code generally masks email and avoids raw provider bodies, but catch blocks that log `Error.message` can inherit database/provider detail. A redaction test and logging policy are needed before the policy can make stronger claims.
- Backups: Neon-managed backup behavior is not configurable or fully documented in this repository. Deletion procedures must address backup aging and restoration.
- Retention: code-enforced ten-minute verification expiry and 30-day resume-token expiry exist; lead and stale rate-limit deletion do not.
- Future marketplace: current registrations do not create accounts, publish profiles, or authorise visibility, automated matching, or recruiter/talent disclosure.
