# ByteSized Careers legal source register

Status: research record for local pre-publication review
Access date for every web source: 17 July 2026

Only official legislation, regulators, or provider primary materials are used as authority. Applicability remains conditional on where users are located, how the service is directed or promoted, campaign practices, and statutory thresholds. This register supports drafting; it is not legal advice.

Operator facts used in the final public drafts: ByteSized Careers is currently operated by Guhan Purushothaman from Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India; the site is limited to people aged 18 or older; and `legal@bytesizedcareers.com` is the privacy, legal, opt-out and grievance contact. The policies give international visitors applicable-law rights without claiming that every foreign framework below applies universally.

## Local primary evidence

The legal analysis also relies on the repository itself as the primary source for current behavior, including:

- `src/lib/db/schema.ts` and migrations for persisted fields;
- `src/lib/actions/*` and `src/lib/validation/*` for collection, validation, and progressive saves;
- `src/lib/email/*` and verification actions for Resend behavior and challenge lifecycle;
- `src/lib/rate-limit/index.ts` for raw-IP handling and peppered buckets;
- `src/lib/utils/resume-storage.ts` and token utilities for localStorage and expiry;
- `src/lib/auth/config.ts`, protected admin pages, and CSV export for GitHub OAuth and access control;
- installed Auth.js source for cookie names/default lifetimes;
- `package.json` and full-repository searches for analytics, telemetry, pixels, monitoring, and provider dependencies.

Provider and regulator materials can change. Re-check this register immediately before publication and before adding a country, communications channel, analytics tool, marketplace feature, or new processor.
